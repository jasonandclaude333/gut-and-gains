FODMAP journey

1. Baseline: log usual meals and symptoms for 3–7 days.

2. Elimination: a dietitian guided low FODMAP phase, usually 2–6 weeks.

3. Reintroduction: test one FODMAP group at a time, recording portions and symptoms.

4. Personalise: keep tolerated foods and identify repeatable triggers.

Check with your GP about coeliac screening before removing gluten. This tracker does not diagnose food intolerance.
